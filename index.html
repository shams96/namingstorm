NamingStorm — AI Business Name Generator & Brand Architect

NamingStorm is an AI-powered business naming platform for startups, e-commerce brands, and consumer goods companies. It engineers category-defining brand names using the NamingStorm Engine, a naming system that runs three distinct strategies on every request: repurposing a familiar word into a new industry, fusing a Latin or Greek root with an invented suffix, and inventing a wholly new, pronounceable brand-vessel word.

Every generated name is scored for phonetic ease of pronunciation and trademark posture (favoring invented or suggestive marks over generic dictionary words), then screened live against the USPTO federal trademark database and checked for real domain availability across .com, .io, .ai, .dev, and .app registries. NamingStorm also generates an investor-ready brand story for the name you choose.

NamingStorm gives every visitor 5 free naming searches before requiring an account. A 10-search pack is available as a one-time purchase, and a Pro subscription unlocks ongoing access to the full naming, scoring, and domain-checking toolkit.
