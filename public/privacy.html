Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

NamingStorm ("we," "us") provides an AI-powered business naming tool at namingstorm.com. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how it's handled.

What We Collect

DataWhenWhy
Name, email, profile photoGoogle Sign-InAccount identification, saving your reports
Phone numberOnly if you opt in via the "text me when ready" promptOptional SMS notification about report status
Product briefs you submit (description, target audience, positioning statement)Every name-generation requestSent to Google's Gemini API to generate names — this is the core function of the product
Names you search for domain/trademark availabilityWhen you use "Check Availability" or "Screen Trademark"Sent to a public domain registry (RDAP) and to MarkerAPI (trademark search) to return results
Feedback submissions (description, optional screenshot, browser info)When you use the "Report an Issue" buttonDebugging and product improvement
Payment informationWhen you purchase a report pack or subscribe to ProProcessed entirely by Stripe — we never see or store your card details ourselves
Guest session dataIf you use the app without signing inAn anonymous, temporary identity tied to your browser only, so guest usage limits and history work

Third Parties We Share Data With

  • Google (Firebase Authentication, Gemini API) — sign-in and AI name generation
  • Stripe — payment processing for paid plans
  • MarkerAPI — preliminary USPTO trademark search (name text only, not sent for guests who never use this feature)
  • Verisign RDAP — public domain-availability lookups (no personal data involved, just the candidate name)

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising or share it with data brokers.

What's Stored Where

  • Firestore (our database): your account record, saved projects, and any domain/trademark acquisitions you initiate — only if you're signed in.
  • Your browser (localStorage/sessionStorage): your shortlisted names, guest session ID, and recent generation history. This stays on your device and is never sent to us unless you're signed in and save it.

Your Choices

  • You can use most of the app as a guest without providing any personal information beyond your product brief.
  • You can skip the phone-number prompt at any time — it's never required.
  • You can request deletion of your account and associated data by emailing us (below).

Trademark Screening Disclaimer

Any trademark or "risk" indication shown in the app is a preliminary, automated screen only — it is not a legal trademark clearance opinion. Always consult a trademark attorney before committing to a name.
